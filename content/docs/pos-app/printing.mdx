# Printing

CloudPOS prints bills, return bills, quotations, kitchen order tickets (KOT), day-close reports and barcode stickers. It prints to thermal receipt printers (112 mm, 80 mm, 58 mm) and to normal page printers or PDF files (A4, A5).

You set printers once per device in **Printer Settings**. What appears on each printout (logo, title, columns, totals, QR code) is set by the admin in **Document Configuration** on the Admin Panel.

## Open Printer Settings

1. Open the side menu.
2. Under **Other**, tap **Printer**. You need the permission `menu.settings.printer.access`.
3. The **Printer Settings** screen opens with the subtitle "Configure printers, paper sizes and receipt themes".

![Printer Settings](media:printer-settings)
*Printer Settings: tabs Billing / Quotation / Kitchen / Barcode printer and PDF Sharing; B2C and B2B billing profiles; paper size and theme (e.g. Bilingual Centered Tax Invoice); Open PDF or a physical printer; Scan for Printers; Resync Doc and Clear Default Printer*

## The tabs

| Tab | Used for | Saved printer | Saved paper size |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Billing Printer** | bills, return bills, order prints from Sales | `default_printer` (B2C), `default_printer_b2b` (B2B) | `default_paper_size`, `default_paper_size_b2b` |
| **Quotation Printer** | quotations | `quotation_printer` | quotation paper size |
| **Kitchen Printer** | kitchen order tickets (KOT) | `kot_printer` | `kot_paper_size` |
| **Barcode Printer** | barcode stickers | `barcode_printer` | sticker layout |
| **PDF Sharing** | PDFs sent by Share or WhatsApp | none (no physical printer) | `share_pdf_paper_size`, `share_pdf_paper_size_b2b` |

Each tab is saved on this device only. Two counters in the same store can use different printers.

## Set up the billing printer

1. Open the **Billing Printer** tab.
2. Choose the segment: **B2C (Retail)** or **B2B (Business)**. B2B uses the B2C settings when you leave it unconfigured.
3. Under **Paper Size Settings**, pick a **Paper Size**: **112mm**, **80mm**, **58mm**, **A5** or **A4**. The app shows "Default paper size saved".
4. Under **Receipt Theme**, pick a **Theme**. Thermal sizes list thermal themes. A4 and A5 list PDF templates. The app shows "Receipt theme saved".
5. Pick the font style: **Font A (Small & Sharp)** (default) or **Font B (Default)**.
6. Under **Available Printers**, tap **Scan for Printers**. The app shows "Scanning for printers..." and then "@count devices found".
7. Tap **Select** next to your printer. The app shows "@device Printer Selected".
8. Tap **Test Print** to print a sample. The app shows "Sample receipt sent to printer".

Where printers come from:

- **Windows:** printers installed in Windows (the print spooler), including USB and network printers.
- **Android and iOS:** Bluetooth printers found nearby. The app asks for **Bluetooth** and **Location** permission first ("Permissions Required").

### Open PDF instead of a printer

When the paper size is **A4** or **A5**, the list also offers **Open PDF**: "Generate and open the @paperSize PDF in your default viewer". Choose it if you do not have a page printer. The app shows "Open PDF selected. A4/A5 documents will open in your PDF viewer." You can print from the PDF viewer.

### Development Printer

When **Developer Mode** is on in Settings, the list also shows **Development Printer**. It saves each printout as an image in a folder instead of printing ("Development Printer selected. Prints will be saved to a folder."). The output folder is shown on screen. Use it for testing layouts without paper.

### Clear Default Printer

**Clear Default Printer** (shown as **Clear** on the PDF Sharing tab) resets this tab's printer, paper size, font and theme to the defaults. The app shows "@type printer settings reset to default".

### Resync Doc

**Resync Doc** downloads the latest document configurations from the server. Use it after the admin changes a template, logo or label in the Admin Panel. The app shows "Document configuration resynced successfully".

## Kitchen printer (KOT)

![Kitchen Printer tab](media:printer-kitchen)
*Printer Settings > Kitchen Printer: KOT paper size (80 mm) and printer, plus Advanced print options (PDF margins, driver behaviour) that apply to all tabs*

1. Open **Kitchen Printer**.
2. Pick the KOT paper size (80 mm in the demo).
3. Scan and **Select** the kitchen printer.

When a restaurant order is sent to the kitchen (**Send Kitchen** or **KOT + BILL**) and no kitchen printer is saved, the app asks you to pick one in a **Print KOT** printer picker. KOT printing must be on (`ENABLE_KOT_PRINT`). The KOT layout uses the **Kitchen Order** document configuration. See [Restaurant and kitchen](/docs/pos-app/restaurant-and-kitchen).

## Barcode printer

1. Open **Barcode Printer** and select the label printer.
2. Adjust the **Barcode Sticker Layout**:
   - **Sticker Size** and **Stickers Per Row**
   - **Gap Between Stickers (mm)** and **Page Margin (mm)**
   - **Barcode Height (pt)**, **Barcode Width (% of sticker)**, **Element Spacing (pt)**
   - **Printer Resolution**, **Print Rotation Correction** (leave at **Printer default** for normal printers)
   - **Invert Print Colors**: use only when the printer prints white on black
   - **Font Sizes** for **Store Name**, **Product Name**, **Price**, **Date**, **Barcode Number**
3. Check the **Sticker Preview**. It is approximate. **Reset** restores defaults.

You print stickers from **Product → Product Barcode**. See [Products and stock](/docs/pos-app/products-and-stock).

## PDF Sharing

**PDF Sharing Settings** — "Configure invoice PDFs independently from physical printers". This tab controls the PDF that is attached when you **Share** an order or confirm with WhatsApp.

- Paper size: **A4** (default) or **A5**.
- Template: any A4/A5 template. Default **Classic**.
- Separate **B2C** and **B2B** profiles. B2B uses the B2C sharing profile when left unconfigured.

## Advanced print options

**Advanced print options** — "Applies to all tabs".

- **Print Margins:** a safe area added to every edge of A4/A5 pages. "Only affects A4 and A5 print jobs." Barcode stickers keep their own **Page Margin**.
- **Printer Driver Settings** (Windows, A4/A5): choose **Use the PDF's selected A4/A5 page size** (recommended for normal invoices, prevents cropping) or **Use the selected printer's saved media settings** (only when the driver is set up for the exact document size).

## Receipt layout and preview

The billing tab has a **Receipt layout & preview** panel: "Check synced labels and preview the printed receipt".

- Sections **Store**, **Invoice**, **Customer**, **Items**, **Totals & bank** and **Footer** list which fields are visible ("@visible/@total visible").
- **Field reference** shows every synced label in **English** and **Arabic** and its **Source**.
- **Live preview** renders a sample receipt with the current settings.
- If nothing shows, the message is "No Bill document configuration is currently synced". Configure the Bill template in the Admin Panel, then tap **Resync**.

## Printing at the counter

- **Confirm and Print (F6)** in billing confirms the sale and prints the bill on the billing printer.
- With `POS_PRINT_DOUBLE_BILL` on, every bill prints twice.
- **Open cash drawer** in billing sends a kick command through the thermal billing printer. If it fails you see "Unable to open cash drawer".
- B2B customers print with the B2B profile (when the `B2B` setting is on).

### Reprint from Sales

![Select Printer](media:print-select-printer)
*Select Printer: paper size, printers found on this PC, and Print Receipt*

1. Open **Sales**, find the order (for example receipt 2-11-261027-0003, backend ORD-004851).
2. Tap **Print** on the row or on the order details page.
3. The **Select Printer** dialog opens. Pick the **Paper Size** and a printer.
4. Tap **Print Receipt**.

If you tap print without a printer, the app asks you to select a printer first.

## Document configurations

The admin controls what each printout contains in **Admin Panel → Document Configuration**. The app downloads them with `GET /api/v1/document/document-configs?store_id=&language=` at startup and on **Resync Doc**, and keeps them on the device for offline printing.

### Document types

| Type | Printed for |
|---|---|
| **Bill** | the sales receipt (thermal) |
| **Bill A4** | the sales bill on A4/A5 |
| **Return Bill** | a sales return |
| **Sales and Return Bill** / **Sales and Return Bill A4** | the sale and its return on one print |
| **Credit Note** | credit note after a return |
| **Invoice** | invoice print and share |
| **Receipt** | payment receipt |
| **Voucher** | customer voucher |
| **Supplier Voucher** | supplier voucher |
| **Quotation** | quotation print |
| **Kitchen Order** | KOT |
| **Barcode** | barcode stickers |
| **Customer Statement** / **Supplier Statement** | ledger statements |

The server also has Delivery Note, Purchase Order, Supplier Invoice, General Payment Voucher, Payslip, Email and ZATCA types used outside the POS screens.

### Fields of a document configuration

| Field | Effect |
|---|---|
| `logo`, `show_logo` | logo image and whether it prints |
| `icon`, `show_icon` | small icon and whether it prints |
| `number_prefix` | prefix of the document number (for example `ORD-` on Bill) |
| `header`, `subheader` | heading lines |
| `terms` | terms printed at the bottom (also used for quotations) |
| `footer` | footer text |
| `template`, `theme` / `active_theme` | template family and default theme |
| `language` | `en`, `ar` or `en_ar` (bilingual). In the demo, Bill is `en_ar`. |
| `item_name`, `unit_name`, `price_name`, `tax_name`, `amount_name` | column titles, chosen from options such as Items/Products/Services, Quantity/Hours, Price/Rate, Tax/GST/VAT/Tax (%), Amount/Total |
| `item_tax_display_mode` | `unit` = tax per unit; `row_total` (default) = tax for the whole line |
| `accent_color`, `font` | styling |
| `is_active` | whether the configuration is used |
| `display_configuration` | the display flags below; each has `visible` and an optional label `value` |

### Display flags

Each flag turns one part of the printout on or off. A flag can also carry its own label text (for example the Arabic column title).

| Area | Flags |
|---|---|
| Store heading | `showStoreName`, `showDescription`, `showTel`, `showEmail`, `showStoreAddress`, `showFssaiInfo`, `showVatNumber`, `showCRNumber`, `showExtraHeading1`, `showExtraHeading2` |
| Invoice | `showInvoiceTitle`, `showInvoiceTitleB2b`, `showInvoiceNumber`, `showDate`, `showTokenNumber` |
| Customer | `showCustomerNameAndPhone`, `showCustomerName`, `showCustomerPhone`, `showCustomerPhoneMasked`, `showCustomerAddress`, `showCustomerVatNumber`, `showCustomerCrNumber`, `showDeliveryMethod`, `showDeliveryPhone`, `showPayment`, `showPaymentBreaked`, `showComment` |
| Item columns | `showSLNumber`, `showParticulars`, `showMRP`, `showQty`, `showRate`, `showRateExcTax`, `showUnit`, `showTaxHeader`, `showRowTotalTaxHeader`, `showDiscountColumn`, `showTotal`, `showWarranty` |
| Totals | `showSubTotal`, `showDiscount`, `showTax`, `showNetAmount`, `showMRPTotal`, `showSaved`, `showAmountInWords`, `showItemsCount`, `showQuantityCount` |
| Customer balance | `showCustomerBalance`, `showCustomerPrevBalance`, `showCustomerPaidAmount`, `showCustomerCurrentBalance` |
| Bank | `showBankInfo`, `showBankName`, `showAccountName`, `showAccountNumber`, `showIBAN`, `showSwiftCode` |
| Footer | `showQRCode`, `showVATFooter`, `showThankYouMessage`, `showTermsConditions`, `showOrderNumberInFooter` |

`showPaymentBreaked` prints the payment breakdown. For the demo sale it shows cash 258.00 and card 400.00 (cash is stored net of the 42.00 change). `showCustomerPhoneMasked` hides part of the phone. `showQRCode` prints the ZATCA QR.

## How it works

- **Server defaults vs your choice.** `GET /api/v1/document/printer-settings` returns the company defaults: `b2c_paper_size`, `b2c_template`, `b2b_paper_size`, `b2b_template`. The app checks them against the allowed `paper_sizes`, `thermal_templates` and `paper_templates`. It applies them only where you have not picked a value yourself on this device (each choice is remembered with a `<key>_user_selected` flag). After **Select Store** at login the defaults are force-applied once.
- **Language.** The document `language` decides English, Arabic or bilingual output. Labels come from the document configuration, not from the app language.
- **Offline.** Document configurations, logos and printer choices are cached, so printing works without internet.
- **Default customer phone.** With `HIDE_DEFAULT_PHONE` on (default), the walk-in customer's phone from `AUTO_ASSIGN_DEFAULT_CUSTOMER` is hidden on prints and PDFs.
- **Title.** `PRINT_TITLE` is the fallback receipt title when the document configuration has none.
- **Customer care.** `COMPANY_CUSTOMER_CARE_PHONE` and `COMPANY_CUSTOMER_CARE_EMAIL` print on some templates and shared PDFs.
- **Public invoice link.** Sharing can send the link `{{server}}/invoice/{orderNumber}` instead of a file.
