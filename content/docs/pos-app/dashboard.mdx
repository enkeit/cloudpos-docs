# Dashboard

The dashboard is the first screen a company admin sees after login. Sales executives open it from the **Dashboard** menu item. It shows sales, money in cash and bank, customers, products, ZATCA status and team activity for the chosen period.

You need the permission `menu.dashboard.main.access` to see the **Dashboard** menu item.

## Which dashboard you get

The app picks the dashboard by your role.

| Role | Dashboard | Title on screen |
|---|---|---|
| `sales_executive`, `restaurant_sales` | Sales executive dashboard | **Sales Executive Dashboard** |
| `supplier` | Supplier dashboard | **Supplier Dashboard** |
| Every other role (for example `company_admin`, `purchase_manager`) | Company admin dashboard | company dashboard |

Only `company_admin` lands on the dashboard straight after login. Other roles land on their work screen (Billing, Restaurant, Attender or Kitchen Master) and open the dashboard from the menu.

## Choosing the period

1. Find the period selector at the top right, next to the date.
2. Pick **Today**, **This Week**, **This Month** or **This Year**.
3. The dashboard reloads every period-based card.

The starting period comes from the company setting `DASHBOARD_DEFAULT_PERIOD` (`today`, `week`, `month` or `year`). If the setting is missing, the dashboard starts on **Today**.

How the dates are worked out:

| Period | Start date | End date |
|---|---|---|
| Today | today | today |
| This Week | 7 days ago | today |
| This Month | first day of this month | today |
| This Year | 1 January this year | today |

## The sales executive dashboard

This is what a sales executive such as salesexecutiv2 at store "متجر النجمة Store" sees. The header says **Welcome, salesexecutiv2!** (or **Welcome back!** if the name is not loaded).

![Sales executive dashboard](media:dashboard)
*Sales Executive Dashboard: failed ZATCA banner, sales overview and company account overview*

The sections, from top to bottom:

### Failed Zatca Invoice banner

A red banner titled **Failed Zatca Invoice** with the subtitle **Tap to review and resend.** and a count.

- It shows only when ZATCA Phase 2 is on (`ZATCA_PHASE_2`) and the count is above 0.
- It shows only if you have the invoice permission `menu.transactions.invoice.access`.
- Tap it to open **Transactions → Invoice**, where you can resend failed invoices.
- In the demo the banner showed **323**.

The count is the number of invoices with ZATCA status failed (`GET /api/v1/invoice/list-invoices?zatca_status=failed`). The banner loads its own count. If the first try fails, it retries once after 5 seconds.

### Sales Overview

Six cards for the chosen period:

| Card | What it shows | Demo (Today) |
|---|---|---|
| **Count** | number of sales in the period | 2 |
| **Amount** | value of sales in the period, with currency | SAR 410 |
| **Customers** | customers who bought in the period | 1 |
| **Products** | total products in the catalogue | 4874 |
| **Revenue** | all-time sales value | SAR 1,866,557.25 |
| **Orders** | all-time order count | 4651 |

These come from `GET /api/v1/dashboard`. The response has `total_sales.today`, `.week`, `.month` and `.year`, each with `total_sales`, `total_customers` and `total_amount`, plus all-time `count` and `total`, and `total_products`.

### Company Account Overview

![Account and activity cards](media:dashboard-activity)
*Dashboard: company account overview and activity overview cards*

Cards for **Bank Account** (money received into bank), **Cash Account** (cash received), **Total Revenue**, **Total Customers** and **Total Orders**.

Data: `GET /api/v1/dashboard/company-overview?start_date=&end_date=`.

### Activity Overview

Small cards: **Catalog Products**, **Products Added Today**, **Products Added This Week**, **Products Added This Month**, **New Orders**, **New Customers**. Same data source as the account overview (`products.*`, `orders.new_orders`, `customers.new_customers`).

### ZATCA Overview and Your Account Overview

![ZATCA overview](media:dashboard-zatca)
*Dashboard: ZATCA overview for invoices and vouchers, and your own cash/bank overview*

**ZATCA Overview** has two groups, **Invoice ZATCA** and **Voucher ZATCA**. Each has four cards:

| Card | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Successfully sent** | accepted by ZATCA |
| **Not sent** | waiting to be submitted |
| **Failed** | needs attention; resend from the invoice or voucher list |
| **Warning** | accepted with warnings; review required |

In the demo, **Not sent** showed 3 invoices. This section shows only when ZATCA Phase 2 is on. Use the refresh icon (**Refresh ZATCA overview**) to reload it. Data: `GET /api/v1/dashboard/zatca-overview` for the chosen period.

**Your Account Overview** shows four cards: **Cash Received**, **Cash Sent**, **Bank Received** and **Bank Sent**.

### Your Sales Performance and customers

![Sales performance](media:dashboard-performance)
*Dashboard: your sales performance, your customers (debit, credit, VVIP) and product overview*

**Your Sales Performance:** **Today's Sales**, **This Week**, **This Month** and **Total Sales**.

**Customers:** **Total Customers**, **Debit Customers** (customers who owe money), **Credit Customers** (customers with balance in their favour) and **Crucial Customers** (VVIP clients). Data: `GET /api/v1/dashboard/customer-stats?period=&start_date=&end_date=`.

### Product Overview and Category Overview

![Product and category overview](media:dashboard-products)
*Dashboard: product breakdown (inactive, sellable, purchasable, taxable) and category overview*

**Product Overview:** **Total Products**, **Active Products**, **Low Stock**, **Total Stock Qty**, **Inactive Products**, **Sellable Products**, **Purchasable Products**, **Taxable Products**, **Non-Taxable Products**, **Products In Stock**.

**Category Overview:** total, sellable, purchasable, taxable and non-taxable categories.

Data: `GET /api/v1/dashboard/products-stats`.

### Works Team, Sales Graph, Orders and Customers

![Graphs](media:dashboard-graphs)
*Dashboard: works team, hourly sales graph and orders & customers graph*

- **Works Team:** the team members returned with the dashboard data (name, email, phone).
- **Sales Graph:** sales over time. It has its own selector: **Today**, **This Week**, **This Month**. Data: `GET /api/v1/dashboard/executive-sales-graph?period=`.
- **Orders & Customers:** monthly orders and new customers for the current year. Data: `GET /api/v1/dashboard/orders-graph?year=` and `GET /api/v1/dashboard/customers-graph?year=`.
- **Sales by Channel:** sales split by **POS**, **Web** and **Kiosk**. Data: `GET /api/v1/dashboard/sales-stats`.
- **Sales Executive Overview:** **Total Executives** and **Sales per Executive** graph. Data: `GET /api/v1/dashboard/executives-overview?start_date=&end_date=`.

### Extra widgets

At the bottom, each with its own refresh button (**Refresh widget**):

| Widget | Cards or columns | Endpoint |
|---|---|---|
| **Orders Summary** | **Orders Today**, **Average Order Amount** | `dashboard/orders-widget` |
| **Latest Orders** | **Order ID**, **Date**, **Customer**, **Status**, **Total** | `dashboard/latest-orders` |
| **Customer Transactions Summary** | **Total Customers**, **Total Credit**, **Total Debit**, **Balance** | `dashboard/customer-transactions-summary` |
| **Revenue Stats** | **Total On Order**, **Credit Collected**, **Credit Sales**, **Total In Bank**, **Total In Cash** | `dashboard/revenue-stats` |
| **Suppliers Graph** | supplier activity graph | `dashboard/suppliers-graph` |

If a widget fails, it shows **Could not load this widget.** The rest of the dashboard keeps working.

## Widget permissions

Every card checks a permission before it shows. Nothing restricted appears until the role list has loaded. If you cannot see a card, ask the admin to tick it in **Admin Panel → Roles → Dashboard Widgets**.

There are two forms:

- Whole widget: `dashboard.widgets.<widget>.access`
- One card: `dashboard.widgets.<widget>.<field>.access`

| Widget | Whole-widget permission | Card permissions used by the app |
|---|---|---|
| Company overview | `dashboard.widgets.company_overview.access` | `bank_account.received_amount`, `bank_account.sent_amount`, `cash_account.received_amount`, `cash_account.sent_amount`, `revenue.total_sales`, `customers.total_customers`, `customers.new_customers`, `orders.total_orders`, `orders.new_orders`, `products.total`, `products.added_today`, `products.added_week`, `products.added_month` |
| Products stats | `dashboard.widgets.products_stats.access` | `total_products`, `active_products`, `low_stock`, `total_products_stock_qty`, `inactive_products`, `sellable_products`, `purchasable_products`, `taxable_products`, `non_taxable_products`, `products_in_stock`, `total_category`, `sellable_category`, `purchasable_category`, `taxable_category`, `non_taxable_category` |
| Customer stats | `dashboard.widgets.customer_stats.access` | `total_customers`, `debit_customers`, `credit_customers`, `crucial_customers` |
| Executives overview | `dashboard.widgets.executives_overview.access` | `total_executives`, `sales_executive_graph` |
| ZATCA overview | `dashboard.widgets.zatca_overview.access` | `success_zatca`, `not_sent`, `failed`, `warning`, `voucher_success`, `voucher_not_sent`, `voucher_failed`, `voucher_warning` |
| Orders widget | `dashboard.widgets.orders_widget.access` | `total_orders_today`, `average_order_amount` |
| Customer transactions summary | `dashboard.widgets.customer_transactions_summary.access` | `total_customers`, `total_credit`, `total_debit`, `total_balance` |
| Suppliers overview | `dashboard.widgets.suppliers_overview.access` | `suppliers.total_suppliers`, `suppliers.new_suppliers` |
| Latest orders | `dashboard.widgets.latest_orders.access` | – |
| Orders graph | `dashboard.widgets.orders_graph.access` | – |
| Customers graph | `dashboard.widgets.customers_graph.access` | – |
| Executive sales graph | `dashboard.widgets.executive_sales_graph.access` | – |
| Sales stats | `dashboard.widgets.sales_stats.access` | – |
| Revenue stats | `dashboard.widgets.revenue_stats.access` | – |
| Suppliers graph | `dashboard.widgets.suppliers_graph.access` | – |
| Suppliers purchase graph | `dashboard.widgets.suppliers_purchase_graph.access` | – |
| Supplier transactions graph | `dashboard.widgets.supplier_transactions_graph.access` | – |
| Supplier credit balance | `dashboard.widgets.supplier_credit_balance.access` | – |

A section is hidden completely when you have none of its card permissions. The backend also defines `dashboard.widgets.section.access` as the parent entry of these widget permissions.

## How it works

- **Figures are server-side.** The dashboard shows what the server returns for the active store and your sign-in. Offline sales that have not synced yet (see **Sales → Confirmed Orders**) are not counted until they reach the server.
- **Reload on period change.** Changing the period always refetches; cached figures are not reused, so the numbers match the selected period.
- **Timeouts.** Each call waits up to 15 seconds. A failed call leaves its section empty or shows **No data available**; the other sections still load.
- **Currency.** Money cards use the company `CURRENCY` setting (SAR in the demo).
- **ZATCA sections** depend on `ZATCA_PHASE_2`. With Phase 2 off, the banner and the ZATCA overview are hidden.
