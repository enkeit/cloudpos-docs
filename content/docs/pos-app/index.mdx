# POS App Overview

CloudPOS POS is the point-of-sale app that store staff use every day. It runs on Windows desktops, Android and iOS tablets, and phones. One app covers supermarket billing, restaurant counters, waiters, the kitchen, stock, purchases, accounts and reports.

The app is built for shops and restaurants in Saudi Arabia and India. It handles tax-inclusive prices, ZATCA e-invoicing (Saudi Arabia), Arabic and English receipts, and works offline when the internet drops.

## Platforms and screen layouts

- **Windows desktop and large tablets.** A sidebar menu on the left. Billing shows the product grid and the cart side by side.
- **Phones.** A slide-out drawer menu grouped into sections (Main, Sales, Inventory, Reports, Accounts, Directory, Settings). Billing uses a bottom bar with **Market**, **Billing**, **Order** and **Cart**.
- **Languages.** English, Arabic (العربية, right-to-left) and Malayalam. Change it in **Settings → Language**.

The app also has a floating **AI** assistant button. Staff can ask it how to do something in the app.

## Who uses the app (roles)

Every user signs in with their own email and password. Their **role** decides which menu items they see and which screen opens after login.

| Role | Typical person | Opens on |
|---|---|---|
| `sales_executive` | Cashier / counter staff | Billing |
| `restaurant_sales` | Restaurant counter cashier | Restaurant |
| `attender` | Waiter taking table orders | Attender |
| `kitchen_master` | Kitchen staff | Kitchen Master |
| `company_admin` | Owner or manager | Dashboard |
| `purchase_manager` | Buyer / storekeeper | Billing |
| `delivery_executive` | Delivery staff (no POS permissions by default) | Billing |

There is no separate "cashier" role. A cashier is a `sales_executive`. The full menu-by-role table is in [Navigation and roles](/docs/pos-app/navigation-and-roles).

## The demo story

All pages use one real demo session as the worked example. The numbers you see in screenshots and steps come from this session.

**Company and store**

- Company **FUNZCART**. Time zone Asia/Riyadh. Currency SAR.
- Store **متجر النجمة Store** (store id 2, Al Hamra). Other stores: **funzcart2** (Al mithnab central, id 32) and **Funzcart Al Zahara** (id 35).
- The signed-in user is a `sales_executive`. The device is **Counter 11**.

**The sale**

1. Billing: **classic chicken bucket** × 2 at 220.00 and **crispy chicken bucket** × 1 at 218.00. MRPs are 250 and 230.
2. Prices include 18% tax. Line tax is 67.12 and 33.25. Net total is **658.00**, tax **100.37**, net without tax **557.63**.
3. A new customer is added: **Layla Al-Harbi**, phone 0551234567, Jeddah, Makkah, B2C. Customer ID 2767.
4. Split payment: card 400 + cash 300 = 700. The 42.00 excess is returned as change. The stored breakdown is cash 258 + card 400 = 658.
5. Confirm in **Finalize Order** with delivery **Store Takeaway**.
6. Result: order **ORD-004851**, receipt number **2-11-261027-0003**, status CONFIRMED, payment Paid. Invoice **INV-OR1002060** is created automatically (ZATCA status NOT SENT). Receipt **RCP1001953**.

**After the sale**

- **Sales return:** 1 × classic chicken bucket returned, 220.00 refunded in cash, reason "Customer changed mind".
- **Quotation QTN-00133** for Layla: 5 × family chicken bucket at 210 = 1050.00, valid for 30 days.
- **Restaurant:** Table 3 orders 1 classic + 1 crispy bucket (438.00). **Send Kitchen** creates **ORD-004852**. The kitchen marks both items Ready, then Served.
- **Day close:** 1 order, total sales 658, expected closing cash 258.
- **Dashboard (today):** 2 sales, SAR 410 revenue, 4874 products, 3 ZATCA invoices not sent.
