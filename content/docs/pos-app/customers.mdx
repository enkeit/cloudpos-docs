# Customers

Every confirmed sale belongs to a customer. The customer record holds contact details, addresses, the account balance, loyalty card and, for business buyers, VAT and CR numbers.

You can work with customers in two places:

- **Customers** in the side menu (permission `menu.customers.main.access`).
- The customer picker in billing (**F3** in Finalize Order).

## Customer list

Open **Customers** from the side menu.

![Customers list](media:customers-list)
*Customers: search as you type by name, email or phone, filter by balance; Layla Al-Harbi B2C with balance 0.00; View opens the profile; export and Add customer*

### Find a customer

1. Type in **Name**, **Email** or **Phone**. Results update as you type.
2. Pick a **Balance** filter: **All**, **Positive (+ve)**, **Negative (-ve)** or **Zero (0)**.
3. Press **Reset** to clear the filters. Use **Show filters** / **Hide filters** to fold the filter box.

### Columns

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **NO.** | Row number |
| **CUSTOMER** | Name and email |
| **BALANCE** | Account balance. Green when 0 or more. Red when below 0 |
| **PHONE** | Main phone |
| **TYPE** | **B2C** or **B2B** |
| **ACTION** | **View** opens the profile |

Other buttons:

- **Add customer** opens the Add New Customer form.
- **Export to Excel** exports the list with the current filters.
- **Refresh customers** reloads the list.

### What the balance means

The balance is total credits minus total debits on the customer's ledger.

- **Negative (red)**: the customer owes the store. Example: a credit sale of 300 leaves −300.
- **Positive (green)**: the store owes the customer. Example: 42.00 kept as customer credit leaves +42.00.
- **Zero**: nothing is due. Layla's balance is 0.00 after her paid order.

## Add a new customer

You can open the form from **Customers → Add customer**, or from the billing customer picker with **Add New Customer**.

The demo customer is Layla Al-Harbi.

1. Fill in **Customer details**.
2. Fill in the **Address**.
3. Fill in **Business details** (shown only when ZATCA Phase 1 is on).
4. Fill in **Account**: opening balance and payment type.
5. Press **Submit**.

![Add customer: details and address](media:customer-add-form)
*Add New Customer: customer details (phone is required and auto-formatted) and address with Pick on Map*

| **Last Name** | No | | Al-Harbi |
| **Email Address** | No | Must be a valid email if entered | layla.demo@funzcart.in |
| **Phone Number** | **Yes** | At least 10 digits. Formatted as you type | 055-123-4567 |
| **Alternate Phone** | No | | |

Errors you may see: "Phone number is required", "Enter a valid phone number", "Enter a valid email address".

*Add New Customer: state, city, pincode, and business details (B2C or B2B, CR number, VAT number)*

### Business details

This section shows only when the `ZATCA_PHASE_1` setting is on. Without it, every new customer is saved as B2C.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Customer Type** | **B2C** (consumer) or **B2B** (business). Layla is B2C |
| **CR Number** | Commercial registration. Required for a new B2B customer ("CR Number is required for B2B") |
| **VAT Number** | VAT registration. Required for a new B2B customer ("VAT Number is required for B2B") |

Why it matters: ZATCA clears B2B invoices and needs the buyer's VAT and CR numbers. B2C invoices are only reported. See [ZATCA](/docs/pos-app/zatca).

### Account (opening balance)

| Field | Notes | Demo value |
|---|---|---|
| **Balance** | Opening balance, up to 2 decimals | 0 |
| **Payment Type** | **To Pay** or **To Receive**. You must pick one ("Please select a payment type") | To Receive |

- **To Receive**: the store will receive this amount from the customer (the customer owes it).
- **To Pay**: the store will pay this amount to the customer (the customer has credit).

![Add customer: account](media:customer-add-account)
*Add New Customer: opening balance with To Pay / To Receive, then Submit*

After **Submit**, the server creates the customer. Layla got customer ID 2767. If you came from billing, the new customer is selected at once.

![New customer selected in billing](media:customer-added)
*New customer Layla Al-Harbi selected automatically, showing her phone and SAR 0.00 balance*

## Customer profile

Press **View** on a row to open **Customer Profile**. The left side has a profile card and these tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Information** | Contact, account and store details |
| **Edit Details** | The same form as Add, to change details |
| **Transactions** | The customer's ledger |
| **All Orders** | The customer's orders |
| **Customer Address** | Saved delivery addresses |
| **Loyalty Card** | Loyalty card and points |
| **Chat** | Messages with the customer |

![Customer profile](media:customer-profile)
*Customer Profile: tabs Information, Edit Details, Transactions, All Orders, Customer Address, Loyalty Card, Chat; contact, account (balance, payment type, loyalty points, member since) and store information*

### Information

- Contact: **Email**, **Phone**, **Alt Phone**, **Gender**, **Date of Birth**.
- Account: **Balance**, **Payment Type**, **Loyalty Points**, **Member Since**, **Total Orders**.
- Store: **Store Name**, **Company ID**.
- Quick actions: **Edit Customer**, **View Orders**, **Message**.

### Edit Details

1. Change the fields you need.
2. Press **Save Changes**. If nothing changed you see a "no changes" message.
3. **Change Password** sends the customer a password reset link (for the online store account).

When editing, **First Name** and **Phone Number** are required. The balance is shown without a sign; the payment type gives the direction.

### Transactions (ledger)

The top strip shows **Total credit** (green), **Total debit** (red) and **Net**. Below it, each ledger line shows **REFERENCE**, **DATE**, **TYPE**, **PAYMENT METHOD**, **AMOUNT**, **STATUS** and **ACTION** (**View**). Filter by **Date range**.

![Customer transactions tab](media:customer-transactions-tab)
*Customer Profile > Transactions: total credit, total debit and net, then each ledger line (invoice debit, receipt credits per payment method, sales return credit, refund voucher debit) with View*

Layla's ledger after the demo:

| Entry | Type | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Invoice INV-OR1002060 for ORD-004851 | Debit | 658.00 |
| Receipt, card | Credit | 400.00 |
| Receipt, cash | Credit | 258.00 |
| Sales return (1 × classic chicken bucket) | Credit | 220.00 |
| Refund voucher, cash | Debit | 220.00 |

Total credit 878.00, total debit 878.00, net 0.00.

How to read it:

- A sale adds a **Debit** (the customer owes the invoice).
- A payment adds a **Credit** (one line per payment method).
- A sales return adds a **Credit**. A cash refund then adds a **Debit**.

### All Orders

Lists the customer's orders with **ORDER #**, **DATE**, **ITEMS**, **TOTAL**, **PAYMENT** and **STATUS**. Open one to see its lines.

### Customer Address

Saved delivery addresses. Each has an **Address Type** (Home, Office or Other), **Address**, **Landmark**, **State**, **District**, **City** and **Pincode**.

1. Add an address, fill the fields, press **Save Address**.
2. Edit one and press **Update Address**.

These addresses appear under **Choose an address:** when you pick Door Delivery in billing.

### Loyalty Card

Shows **Card Number**, **Membership**, **Membership Code**, **Valid From**, **Valid Until**, **Card Status**, **Loyalty Points**, **Min Redeemable Points** and **Price Per Point**. If there is no card you see "No loyalty card information available".

The company prop `PER_POINT_PRICE` (default 100) sets how much spend earns one point.

### Chat

A message thread with the customer. Type a message and press send.

## Customers in billing

- The header shows the selected customer's name, phone and **Balance**.
- Finalize Order shows **Previous balance** in the order summary.
- When `SHOW_CUSTOMER_LAST_BUYED_PRICE_LIST` is on, each cart row has a history icon (or press **H**). It shows the last prices this customer paid for the product. **Use This** applies an old price.
- When `AUTO_ASSIGN_DEFAULT_CUSTOMER` is on, a walk-in customer is chosen for you. Purchase history is not shown for that customer.
- When `SKIP_CUSTOMER_SELECTION` is on, the customer step in checkout is skipped.

See [Billing](/docs/pos-app/billing).

## Other customer reports

- **Party Accounts → Customer Transactions**: every customer debit and credit across all customers. See [Transactions and accounts](/docs/pos-app/transactions-and-accounts).
- **Reports → Customer Transaction Reports**: total debit, credit and balance per customer. See [Reports](/docs/pos-app/reports).

## How it works

- The list and the billing picker both use `customer/customer-searchbar`. The search covers name, email and phone.
- New customers are created with `customer/add-customer` in the active store. The fields sent include `phone` (digits only), `name` (first + last), `email`, `address`, `pincode`, `city` (district id), `state` (state id), `country`, `balance`, `payment_type` (`to_pay` or `to_receive`), `alt_phone`, `gender`, `dob`, `customer_type` (`B2C` or `B2B`), `cr_number`, `vat_number`.
- Edits use `customer/customer-edit` and send only the changed fields.
- Addresses use `customer/executive-add-address` and `customer/executive-update-address`.
- States, districts and pincodes come from `location/get-states`, `location/get-district` and `location/list-pincodes`.
- The ledger comes from `transaction/list-transactions?customer_id=…`. Balance = sum of credits − sum of debits.
- The last purchase prices come from `customer/customer-last-purchases`.
- Customers are cached on the device for offline billing (Settings → Offline Data).
